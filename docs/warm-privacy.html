Privacy Policy

Warm ("the App") is a thermal therapy utility that uses your device's CPU and GPU to generate heat. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not use tracking technologies of any kind.

Device permissions used

  • Wake Lock — keeps the screen on during a session so the device does not sleep mid-therapy. No data is read or transmitted.
  • Battery status — read locally to auto-stop the session when the battery is critically low (≤ 15 %). This value is never stored or sent anywhere.

Local storage

The App stores a single boolean flag in the device's local storage (warm_premium_v1) to remember whether you have purchased the Premium upgrade. This value never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases

Premium features are sold as a one-time purchase through Google Play. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive or store any payment or billing information.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the date below and publish the updated version at the same URL. Continued use of the App after any change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@warm.app.
